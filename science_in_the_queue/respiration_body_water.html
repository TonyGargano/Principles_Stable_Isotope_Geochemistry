Breathing into Body Water

Companion to Inhale, Exhale on Science in the Queue. A steady-state oxygen mass balance that follows the O₂ an animal takes up, the CO₂ it breathes out and the vapor it exchanges into body water, in δ′¹⁸O and Δ′¹⁷O together.

Published sources behind the parameters, alveolar and whole-breath extraction in mammals (Beaver et al. 1981; Stahl 1967; Chappell and Roverud 1990), parabronchial and whole-breath extraction in birds (Johansen and Bech 1983; Johannesen and Nicol 1990; Bech et al. 1985; Chappell and Bucher 1987), exhaled-air temperature (Schmidt-Nielsen et al. 1970; Cole 1954; Tieleman et al. 1999), intermittent breathing in reptiles (Glass et al. 1978; Bennett and Hicks 2001), water economy indices (Nagy 2004), end-expired O₂ in dolphins (Fahlman et al. 2018, 2019), and hibernation energetics and torpor (Karpovich et al. 2009; Geiser 2004; Webb and Milsom 2017). RQ follows fuel stoichiometry, 1.00 for carbohydrate, 0.85 mixed, about 0.74 for protein to uric acid and 0.703 for fat. Values marked assumed are constructed. Water influx is per O atom of O₂ taken up, from the water economy index (WEI) through the energy released per mole of O₂.

Published inputs, air O₂ at δ¹⁸O 23.881‰ and Δ′¹⁷O −0.440‰, the uptake relation of Epstein and Zeiri (1988) in the mass-balance form of Kohn (1996), the residual-O₂ slope 0.518 for human breath (Luz and Barkan 2005), which my breath holds bracket, the CO₂–water fractionation of Brenninkmeijer et al. (1983), and liquid–vapor fractionation with θ 0.529. Measured, z of 10.4‰ from my breath holds. Constructed, the six archetypes, with the assumed values marked in the parameter table above, all oxygen other than O₂ fixed at δ′¹⁸O −5‰ and Δ′¹⁷O +0.015‰ for every animal so that only breathing differs, and every number the model returns. The path switches the terms on one at a time, so its last point is the exact body water. Moving f rescales the water influx, and RQ and body temperature then act at that water economy.
